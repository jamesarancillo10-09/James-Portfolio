Work

Featured Automation Projects

Selected workflows from my hands-on automation work and training — not invented client case studies.

Lead Enrichment & Qualification

Problem— New leads require repetitive enrichment, qualification, routing, and follow-up.
Solution— Zapier enriches each lead, separates high- and low-priority paths, logs data, alerts the team, and prepares personalized email communication.
Result— Faster lead response, more consistent routing, and less manual lead handling.
Zapier Typeform Webhooks Apollo Paths Google Sheets Slack Gmail

AI Content Repurposing

Problem— Turning one source file into content for several channels involves repeated transcription, writing, and publishing tasks.
Solution— Zapier pulls files from Google Drive, uses AI for transcription and blog creation, then distributes content through Paths and Looping.
Result— Less repetitive content processing and more consistent multi-channel distribution.
Google Drive AI Facebook Pages LinkedIn Instagram Paths Looping

CRM Lead Engagement

Problem— Leads at different CRM stages need different follow-up, making manual tracking inconsistent.
Solution— Zapier uses Asana stages, Paths, Filters, AI-generated messages, Gmail, and delays to trigger the appropriate follow-up.
Result— More consistent stage-based communication with less manual monitoring.
Asana Zapier Paths Filters Gmail Google Drive Delay

Gmail Attachment & AI File Processing

Problem— Email attachments require repeated downloading, reviewing, storing, and data entry.
Solution— Make.com watches Gmail, processes attachments with AI, stores files in Google Drive, maps data to Google Sheets, and applies conditional logic.
Result— Better-organized files and data with fewer repetitive handling tasks.
Make.com Gmail AI Google Drive Google Sheets Data Mapping

Asana–Xero Integration

Problem— Moving task data and attachments between Asana and Xero manually creates repeated data-handling work.
Solution— Make.com connects both platforms through APIs, using routers, iterators, Google Sheets logging, and attachment handling.
Result— Less repetitive data entry and more consistent organization across the connected tools.
Make.com Asana Xero APIs Routers Iterators

n8n AI Automation Training

Problem— Building flexible n8n automations requires practical knowledge of nodes, data handling, APIs, branching, and looping.
Solution— This hands-on training project applies those concepts through AI Agents, AI workflows, APIs, MCP, and workflow logic.
Result— Demonstrates practical n8n workflow-building skills without presenting the training as client work.
n8n AI Agents APIs MCP Workflow Nodes
